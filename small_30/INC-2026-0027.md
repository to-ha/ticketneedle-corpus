## Symptom
The message broker queue depth began to grow unbounded, indicating a potential issue with the build artifact registry. This symptom was first observed by monitoring tools, which alerted the team to investigate further. As the queue depth continued to increase, it became clear that the issue was causing a significant impact on the platform's performance.

Further investigation revealed that the growing queue depth was likely related to an issue with the registry's connection limit. The team suspected that the registry might be experiencing difficulties in handling the volume of requests, leading to a backlog of unprocessed messages.

## Escalation timeline
- 13:38 — L1 Platform support: Liu began reviewing the monitoring alerts and initial logs to understand the scope of the issue.
- 13:59 — L2 Platform engineer: Ahmed took over the investigation, focusing on the message broker and its interaction with the build artifact registry.
- 14:21 — L3 Platform lead: Imani joined the effort, bringing expertise on the registry's configuration and performance tuning.

## Diagnosis steps and hypotheses
Initial hypotheses focused on potential misconfigurations or performance issues within the message broker itself. However, as the investigation progressed, attention turned to the build artifact registry due to its critical role in the platform's infrastructure. A key diagnostic step involved checking the registry's health and connectivity:
```
curl -v http://localhost:5000/v2/_catalog
```
This command helped verify whether the registry was reachable and functioning as expected, providing valuable insight into the nature of the problem.

## Resolution steps
1. Restart registry service on node 3
2. Set max_connections to 50 in /etc/registry/config.yml
3. Clear cache at /var/lib/registry/cache
4. Update broker-admin flag --registry-retry-count to 3

## Post-mortem note
The build artifact registry exceeded its connection limit. This root cause was identified through a combination of diagnostic efforts and analysis of the registry's configuration and performance metrics. The applied resolution steps effectively addressed the issue, restoring normal operation to the platform.
