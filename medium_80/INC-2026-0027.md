# Major Incident — INC-2026-0027
- **Ticket ID:** INC-2026-0027

- **Domain:** Platform infrastructure
- **Status:** Resolved

## Symptom
The message broker queue depth began to grow unbounded, indicating a potential issue with the build artifact registry. This symptom was first observed by monitoring tools, which alerted the team to investigate further. As the queue depth continued to increase, it became clear that the issue was causing a significant impact on the platform's performance.

Further investigation revealed that the growing queue depth was likely related to the build artifact registry's inability to process requests efficiently. The team suspected that there might be a connection limit or resource constraint that was preventing the registry from functioning correctly.

## Escalation timeline
- 13:38 — L1 Platform support: Liu began reviewing the monitoring alerts and initial logs to understand the scope of the issue.
- 13:59 — L2 Platform engineer: Ahmed took over the investigation, focusing on the message broker and build artifact registry interaction.
- 14:21 — L3 Platform lead: Imani joined the effort, bringing expertise on the platform's infrastructure and potential bottlenecks.

## Diagnosis steps and hypotheses
Initial hypotheses suggested that the issue might be related to network connectivity or firewall rules. However, after running diagnostic commands such as 
```
curl -v http://localhost:5000/v2/_catalog
```
it became clear that the build artifact registry was reachable but not performing as expected. The team then shifted focus towards resource constraints and connection limits.

## Resolution steps
1. Restart registry service on node 3
2. Set max_connections to 50 in /etc/registry/config.yml
3. Clear cache at /var/lib/registry/cache
4. Update broker-admin flag --registry-retry-count to 3

## Post-mortem note
The build artifact registry exceeded its connection limit. This incident highlighted the importance of regularly reviewing configuration settings and resource allocations to prevent similar issues in the future.
