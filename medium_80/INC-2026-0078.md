## Symptom
The incident began with reports of ingress returning 502 errors for a single backend service. This symptom was consistently observed across multiple user sessions and geographic locations, indicating a potential issue with the service's availability or configuration. Further investigation revealed that the errors were not intermittent, suggesting a persistent problem rather than a transient issue.

Upon closer inspection, it became clear that the affected backend service relied on the etcd cluster for configuration and service discovery. The consistent nature of the 502 errors pointed towards a possible issue with the etcd cluster's health or connectivity.

Initial troubleshooting steps focused on verifying the service's deployment and network connectivity, but these did not yield any obvious issues. The focus then shifted towards the etcd cluster, given its critical role in the service's operation.

- 03:31 — L2 Platform engineer: Mateo took over, diving deeper into the etcd cluster's logs and health status to look for signs of trouble.

## Diagnosis steps and hypotheses
Initial hypotheses centered around potential misconfigurations or connectivity issues between the backend service and the etcd cluster. However, as the investigation progressed, attention turned to the health of the etcd cluster itself. A key command used in the diagnosis was:
```
kubectl get etcdcluster -o yaml
```
This command provided insight into the current state and configuration of the etcd cluster, helping to identify potential issues with member status or quorum.

## Resolution steps
1. Restart etcd node 2 at 10.0.0.5
2. Set etcd quorum threshold to 3
3. Remove failed member with etcdctl member remove 1234
4. Update etcd cluster config with helm upgrade --set etcd.replicaCount=5

## Post-mortem note
The etcd cluster became unavailable due to a quorum loss. This incident highlights the importance of closely monitoring the etcd cluster's health and taking proactive measures to prevent quorum losses, such as ensuring adequate node counts and configuring appropriate thresholds.
