## Diagnosis steps and hypotheses
The team considered several hypotheses to explain the MTU black hole, including misconfigured MPLS labels, incorrect route reflector settings, and issues with VPNv4 route exchange. To investigate these possibilities, the team used a variety of diagnostic tools, including Wireshark and dig. One key command used during the diagnosis was:
```
tcpdump -i any -n -vv -s 0 -c 100 port 179
```
This command helped to capture and analyze BGP traffic, providing valuable insights into the network's routing behavior. Although the initial hypotheses were later ruled out, the diagnostic process helped to narrow down the potential causes and ultimately identify the root cause of the incident.

## Resolution steps
1. Set MPLS label range to 16000-17000
2. Restart L3VPN service on router 10.1.1.1
3. Update route reflector IP to 10.2.2.2 port 5000
4. Verify VPNv4 route exchange with dig +short 10.3.3.3
