# Major Incident — INC-2026-0044
* **Ticket ID:** INC-2026-0044
* **Reported:** 2026-02-26 14:49 UTC
* **Priority:** P2
* **Domain:** Monitoring
* **Status:** Resolved

## Symptom
The incident began with reports of errors from real users, despite synthetic probes indicating that the system was functioning correctly. This discrepancy suggested a problem with the log aggregation forwarder, which was responsible for collecting and processing logs from various sources. Further investigation revealed that the forwarder was indeed experiencing issues, leading to a backlog of unprocessed logs.

As the incident progressed, it became clear that the errors seen by real users were related to the forwarder's inability to handle the volume of incoming logs. The synthetic probes, on the other hand, were not affected by this issue, as they were not reliant on the forwarder for their functionality. This highlighted the importance of monitoring the forwarder's performance and adjusting its configuration as needed to prevent similar incidents in the future.

The symptom complex was characterized by a growing backlog of unprocessed logs, which in turn caused errors for real users. This was the primary observable pattern that prompted further investigation and eventual resolution of the incident.

## Diagnosis steps and hypotheses
Initial hypotheses focused on potential issues with the log aggregation forwarder's configuration or performance. To investigate these hypotheses, the `tsdb-cli` tool was used to inspect the forwarder's configuration and performance metrics. Specifically, the following command was executed:
```
tsdb-cli inspect --config /etc/tsdb.conf
```
This command provided valuable insights into the forwarder's configuration and helped identify potential bottlenecks. Although the initial hypotheses were not entirely accurate, they led to a deeper understanding of the forwarder's behavior and ultimately contributed to the resolution of the incident.

## Resolution steps
1. Restart log forwarder with --max-connections 500
2. Set buffer_size to 1048576 in /etc/log-forwarder.conf
3. Increase timeout to 30s for remote_write in tsdb configuration

## Post-mortem note
The log aggregation forwarder was overwhelmed by incoming logs due to insufficient buffer size. This incident highlights the importance of regularly reviewing and adjusting the forwarder's configuration to ensure it can handle the expected volume of logs.
