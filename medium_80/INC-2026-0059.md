## Symptom
The monitoring system alerted on a trace sampler rate misconfiguration, indicating that critical spans were missing. This issue was first observed in the log aggregation forwarder, where gaps in the logs suggested that some events were not being properly forwarded. Upon further investigation, it became clear that this was not an isolated problem, but rather a symptom of a deeper issue affecting the entire monitoring domain.

The missing critical spans meant that the system was unable to accurately track and analyze certain events, leading to incomplete data and potentially incorrect insights. This had significant implications for the team's ability to troubleshoot and resolve issues in a timely manner.

## Diagnosis steps and hypotheses
Initial hypotheses focused on potential configuration issues or network connectivity problems. However, as the team dug deeper, they considered the possibility of a buffer overflow causing the log aggregation forwarder to fail. To investigate this further, the team used the following command:
```
log-tail -f /var/log/log_forwarder.log
```
This allowed them to monitor the log output in real-time and look for any signs of buffering issues or other anomalies.

## Resolution steps
1. Restart log-forwarder service on node 3
2. Set buffer_size to 1024 in /etc/log_forwarder.conf
3. Increase timeout to 30s in /etc/log_forwarder.conf

## Post-mortem note
The log aggregation forwarder failed due to buffer overflow. This incident highlights the importance of regularly reviewing and adjusting configuration settings to prevent similar issues from arising in the future.
