# Major Incident — INC-2026-0096
* **Ticket ID:** INC-2026-0096
* **Reported:** 2026-03-01 15:22 UTC
* **Priority:** P3
* **Domain:** Platform infrastructure
* **Status:** Resolved

## Symptom
The CI runner pool experienced a significant disruption, causing secret rotation to break for one downstream consumer. This resulted in authentication failures and interrupted service. Monitoring detected an unusual spike in concurrent job requests, overwhelming the pool's capacity. As a consequence, jobs were queued indefinitely, leading to timeouts and errors.

## Escalation timeline
* 15:28 — L1 Platform support: Maya - Maya began investigating the issue by reviewing runner logs for any error patterns.
* 15:53 — L2 Platform engineer: Iris - Iris took over the investigation, focusing on the CI runner pool's configuration and performance metrics.

## Diagnosis steps and hypotheses
Initial hypotheses suggested a configuration issue or a resource constraint. To explore these leads, the `registry-gc` tool was utilized to analyze the registry's health.
```
registry-gc -v
```
However, the output did not indicate any issues with the registry itself. Further investigation into the runner logs and job queues pointed towards an overload of the CI runner pool as the primary cause.

## Resolution steps
1. Restart runner with --max-retries 5
2. Set registry_gc_interval to 30m
3. Run broker-admin with --purge-inactive
4. Check runner-logs for error patterns
5. Adjust ci_runner_pool_size to 10

## Post-mortem note
The CI runner pool was overwhelmed by too many concurrent jobs. This incident highlights the importance of closely monitoring the CI runner pool's performance and adjusting its configuration according to the workload demands to prevent similar disruptions in the future.
