## Symptom
The CI pipelines stall on the same stage across all repos, indicating a potential issue with the secrets manager. This symptom first appeared at 02:15 UTC and was reported to the L1 Platform support team at 02:19 UTC. The pipelines were unable to retrieve the necessary secrets, causing them to hang indefinitely. As the incident progressed, it became clear that the issue was not specific to a particular repository or pipeline, but rather a global problem affecting all CI workflows. The user impact was significant, with multiple teams unable to deploy their applications or perform routine maintenance tasks.

The monitoring system caught the issue through alerts on pipeline failures, which triggered an initial investigation into the cause of the stalls. However, the root cause was not immediately apparent, and further diagnosis was required to identify the problem. The fact that all pipelines were affected suggested a common dependency or infrastructure issue, rather than a problem with individual pipelines or repositories.

As the incident continued, the team began to notice that the secrets manager was experiencing intermittent connectivity issues, which may have contributed to the pipeline stalls. However, this was not the primary cause of the problem, and further investigation was needed to determine the root cause. The team also observed that the secrets manager's database was experiencing high latency, which may have been related to the issue.

The symptom complex was characterized by a complete failure of CI pipelines to progress beyond a certain stage, with no errors or warnings indicating a specific cause. This suggested a problem with a shared dependency or infrastructure component, rather than a issue with individual pipelines or repositories. The team's initial hypothesis was that the secrets manager was experiencing a technical issue, but this would need to be confirmed through further diagnosis.

The incident had significant business impact, as multiple teams were unable to deploy their applications or perform routine maintenance tasks. ## Diagnosis steps and hypotheses
The team initially suspected that the issue might be related to a recent change in the secrets manager's configuration. To investigate this hypothesis, they reviewed the configuration files and checked for any recent updates or modifications. However, no changes were found, and this hypothesis was ruled out.

Next, the team considered the possibility of a network connectivity issue between the pipelines and the secrets manager. They used tools like `dig` and `tcpdump` to verify the connectivity and found no issues, which led them to rule out this hypothesis as well.

Another hypothesis was that the secrets manager's database might be experiencing performance issues, leading to the pipeline stalls. The team used the `vault read -format=json /secret/path` command to test the database's responsiveness:
```
vault read -format=json /secret/path
```
However, the response times were within normal limits, and this hypothesis was also ruled out.

The team then suspected that there might be an issue with the secrets manager's caching mechanism, causing the pipelines to stall. They used the `registry-gc` tool to inspect the cache and found no issues, which led them to rule out this hypothesis as well.

As the diagnosis continued, the team considered the possibility of a problem with the secrets manager's lease renewal process. They used the `broker-admin` tool to investigate the lease renewals and found some inconsistencies, which suggested that this might be the root cause of the issue.

After ruling out several hypotheses, the team finally identified the root cause of the issue: the secrets manager's database had become inconsistent due to a failed lease renewal. This was causing the pipelines to stall, as they were unable to retrieve the necessary secrets.

## Resolution steps
1. Stop vault service on node 3
2. Run registry-gc with --debug flag
3. Set VAULT_ADDR to https://localhost:8200
4. Use broker-admin to revoke leases older than 3600s
5. Restart vault service with --log-level=DEBUG

## Post-mortem note
The secrets manager database became inconsistent due to a failed lease renewal. This issue was missed initially because the team was focused on other potential causes, such as configuration changes or network connectivity issues. In hindsight, the team should have investigated the lease renewal process earlier, as it was a critical component of the secrets manager's functionality. To prevent similar incidents in the future, the team will implement additional monitoring and logging for the lease renewal process, to ensure that any issues are caught promptly. The team will also review the secrets manager's configuration and procedures to identify areas for improvement and reduce the risk of similar incidents occurring in the future.
