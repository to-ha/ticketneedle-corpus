## Symptom
The application connection pool began reporting periodic spikes of dropped connections, with the pattern repeating every 30-45 minutes and clearing within a minute. This symptom was first observed during a release window when deadlocks spiked, indicating a potential issue with the database's ability to manage connections efficiently. Further investigation showed that these spikes were not directly related to the release itself but rather to an underlying mechanism affecting connection pool stability.

The observable pattern of dropped connections suggested an issue with how the database handled idle connections or the autovacuum daemon's interaction with the connection pool. Monitoring data indicated that the spikes in dropped connections correlated with increased activity from the autovacuum daemon, hinting at a possible link between vacuum operations and connection pool management.

Given the periodic nature of the issue, it was crucial to identify what database processes or settings could be contributing to these spikes. The focus was on understanding how the autovacuum daemon and vacuum processes might interfere with the connection pool's operation, particularly during periods of high activity.

## Diagnosis steps and hypotheses
Initial hypotheses centered around the autovacuum daemon's activity and its potential to cause deadlocks or interfere with the connection pool. To investigate this, the `psql` command-line tool was used to inspect database activity, including running queries like:
```
psql -c "SELECT * FROM pg_stat_activity WHERE state = 'idle'"
```
This helped identify idle connections that might be affected by the autovacuum daemon. However, the results did not directly point to the root cause, suggesting that the issue was more complex and related to how the pooler service managed connections during vacuum operations.

## Resolution steps
1. Set pool_resize_grace_window to 60s
2. Disable health_check_kill_idle_connections
3. Restart pooler service with new config
4. Verify idle connection eviction with pg_stat_activity

## Post-mortem note
Pooler health-check killed idle backends mid-transaction during a graceful pool resize. This root cause explained the periodic spikes in dropped connections, as the health check's aggressive behavior during pool resizing led to unnecessary connection terminations. Understanding and addressing this issue will help prevent similar incidents in the future.
