## Symptom
During the release window, the database experienced a significant spike in deadlocks. This increase in deadlocks led to a rise in transaction failures and errors, impacting the overall performance of the database. As a result, users encountered delays and timeouts when attempting to access or update data.

The monitoring system alerted the team to the abnormal spike in deadlocks, prompting an investigation into the cause. Further analysis revealed that the deadlocks were primarily occurring on the read-replica pair, suggesting a potential issue with data synchronization or connection management.

## Diagnosis steps and hypotheses
Initial hypotheses centered around issues with the database's configuration, particularly settings that could affect the read-replica pair's ability to synchronize data efficiently. The team considered the possibility of network connectivity problems or disk I/O bottlenecks but found no evidence to support these theories. To gain more insight into the database's current state, the team executed the following command:
```
psql -c 'SELECT * FROM pg_stat_activity;'
```
This command provided valuable information about the current connections and activities within the database, helping to narrow down the potential causes of the deadlocks.

## Resolution steps
1. Restart replica on host db-replica-1
2. Set max_connections to 100 in postgresql.conf
3. Adjust wal_receiver_timeout to 60 seconds

## Post-mortem note
The read-replica pair failed to synchronize due to exhausted connections. This incident highlighted the importance of closely monitoring database performance metrics, especially during release windows when additional load is expected. Implementing more proactive measures to prevent connection exhaustion will be a focus for future improvements.
