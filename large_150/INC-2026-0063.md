## Symptom
The object storage bucket replication lag began to grow past the SLA threshold, indicating a potential issue with the replication process. This was first observed by monitoring tools, which triggered an alert when the lag exceeded the acceptable limit. Further investigation revealed that the replication lag had been increasing steadily over the past few hours, suggesting a gradual deterioration of the system's performance.

As the incident unfolded, it became clear that the growing replication lag was affecting the availability of data across different storage nodes. This, in turn, impacted the overall performance of applications relying on the object storage system. The symptom complex was characterized by a single, coherent issue: the object replication lag growing past the SLA threshold.

The monitoring tools provided detailed metrics on the replication lag, allowing the engineering team to track the issue's progression and understand its impact on the system. The focus was on identifying the root cause of the increasing lag and resolving it before it led to more severe consequences, such as data inconsistencies or complete service unavailability.

## Diagnosis steps and hypotheses
Initial hypotheses suggested a network connectivity issue or a problem with the storage nodes themselves. However, upon further investigation, these were ruled out based on evidence from network packet captures and node health checks. A key command used in the diagnosis process was:
```
rsync --dry-run -avz /mnt/primary/bucket1/
```
This helped in understanding the replication process's behavior without actually transferring data, providing insights into potential bottlenecks or configuration issues.

## Resolution steps
1. Run rsync --dry-run -avz /mnt/primary/bucket1/
2. Set replication.delay to 300 in /etc/object-storage.conf
3. Restart object-storage service on node3
4. Verify disk health with smartctl -H /dev/sda

## Post-mortem note
The object storage bucket replication failed due to high disk latency. This incident highlighted the importance of closely monitoring disk performance and adjusting configuration settings as needed to prevent similar issues in the future.
