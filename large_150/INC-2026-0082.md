# Major Incident — INC-2026-0082
- **Ticket ID:** INC-2026-0082
- **Reported:** 2026-01-28 01:24 UTC
- **Priority:** P1
- **Domain:** Authentication
- **Status:** Resolved

## Symptom
Users reported being unable to log in to various applications due to authentication failures. The error messages indicated that the SAML assertions were rejected as expired, despite the clocks being in sync across all systems. This issue was first noticed by users attempting to access a critical business application, which relies on OIDC for authentication. Further investigation showed that the problem was not limited to a single application but was a widespread issue affecting all services that use the OIDC identity provider for user authentication.

The symptom complex revolved around the expiration of SAML assertions, which suggested an issue with time synchronization or the validation of these assertions by the identity provider. However, given that clock sync issues were ruled out, the focus shifted towards the validation process and potential problems with the identity provider's configuration or certificates.

## Diagnosis steps and hypotheses
Initial hypotheses included a clock synchronization issue between the service providers and the identity provider, despite initial reports suggesting this was not the case. Another hypothesis considered was a misconfiguration in the OIDC settings that could lead to the premature expiration of SAML assertions. To diagnose the issue further, the `openssl s_client -connect idp.example.com:443` command was used to check the identity provider's certificate validity and any potential issues with the TLS connection.
```
openssl s_client -connect idp.example.com:443
```
This helped in identifying a potential certificate-related issue, which aligned with the hypothesis of a configuration or certificate problem at the identity provider.

## Resolution steps
1. Set OIDC issuer URL to https://idp.example.com
2. Update client ID to 1234567890 in /etc/oidc/config.json
3. Restart auth service with --debug flag
4. Verify token signature with openssl s_client -connect idp.example.com:443

## Post-mortem note
The OIDC identity provider certificate expired yesterday. This incident highlights the importance of proactive monitoring of certificate expiration dates for critical infrastructure components like the OIDC identity provider, to prevent such authentication failures in the future.
