# Major Incident — INC-2026-0138
* **Ticket ID:** INC-2026-0138
* **Reported:** 2026-03-19 14:56 UTC
* **Priority:** P3
* **Domain:** Platform infrastructure
* **Status:** Resolved

## Symptom
The CI pipelines began to stall on the same stage across all repositories, indicating a potential issue with the secrets manager. This symptom was first observed by the monitoring team, who noticed a significant increase in pipeline failures. Further investigation revealed that the pipelines were unable to retrieve necessary secrets, causing them to hang indefinitely. The development team reported similar issues when attempting to manually trigger pipeline runs.

## Escalation timeline
* 15:08 — L1 Platform support: Yuki - Yuki attempted to restart the affected pipelines but was unsuccessful.
* 15:20 — L2 Platform engineer: Ahmed - Ahmed began investigating the secrets manager configuration and logs for any errors or misconfigurations.

## Diagnosis steps and hypotheses
Initial hypotheses pointed towards a network connectivity issue or a problem with the secrets manager's database. However, after running `kubectl logs secrets-manager -c config-init`, it became clear that the issue was related to the secrets manager's initialization process. The logs indicated that the secrets manager was unable to initialize due to a configuration error.

## Resolution steps
1. Set max_retries to 5 in secrets.conf
2. Restart secrets manager on node 3
3. Update flag SECRETS_MANAGER_ENABLED to true
4. Verify config at /etc/secrets/config.yaml

## Post-mortem note
The secrets manager failed to initialize due to a misconfigured retry limit. This incident highlights the importance of regularly reviewing and updating configuration files to prevent similar issues in the future.
