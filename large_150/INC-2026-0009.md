## Symptom
The incident began with reports of the log forwarder experiencing back-pressure, resulting in events being dropped silently. This issue was first observed through monitoring alerts, which indicated a significant increase in dropped events over a short period. Further investigation revealed that the tracing collector was not forwarding logs as expected, leading to a buildup of events in the log forwarder's buffer.

As the incident progressed, the monitoring team noticed that the number of dropped events continued to rise, suggesting a problem with the tracing collector's ability to handle the incoming log volume. The team attempted to verify this hypothesis by checking the tracing collector's status and configuration but found no immediate issues.

The symptom complex was characterized by the silent dropping of events, which made it challenging to diagnose the root cause without additional tools and investigation. The monitoring team relied on tools like `log-tail` and `tsdb-cli` to understand the scope of the issue and identify potential causes.

## Diagnosis steps and hypotheses
Initial hypotheses focused on network connectivity issues or misconfigurations in the tracing collector. However, these were quickly ruled out as the team verified that the collector was reachable and its configuration appeared correct. The team then considered the possibility of a buffer overflow due to high traffic, which would explain the silent dropping of events.

To investigate this hypothesis, the team used the `trace-query` tool to inspect the tracing collector's buffer size and performance:
```
trace-query -p 55678 -b 1024
```
This command provided insight into the collector's current state and helped identify the root cause of the issue.

## Resolution steps
1. Restart tracing collector on port 55678
2. Set buffer size to 1024 in /etc/trace/config.yaml
3. Adjust timeout to 30s in /etc/trace/config.yaml

## Post-mortem note
The tracing collector buffer overflowed due to high traffic. This incident highlights the importance of monitoring the tracing collector's performance and adjusting its configuration as needed to prevent similar issues in the future.
