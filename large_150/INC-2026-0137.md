## Symptom
The metrics ingestion pipeline issue became apparent when synthetic probes reported green, indicating no issues, while real users were experiencing errors. This discrepancy suggested a problem with the metrics data being ingested, as the synthetic probes rely on this data to report their status. Further investigation revealed that the error rates for real users were significantly higher than what the synthetic probes were indicating.

Upon reviewing the alert history, it became clear that the issue was not with the probes themselves but with the data they were receiving. The log-tail tool showed no obvious errors in the probe logs, reinforcing the idea that the problem lay elsewhere in the metrics pipeline. 

The focus then shifted to understanding why real users were seeing errors despite the synthetic probes reporting all clear. This led to a deeper dive into the metrics ingestion process to identify any potential bottlenecks or misconfigurations.

## Escalation timeline
* 09:23 — L1 SRE on-call: Nina began reviewing the initial reports and alert history to understand the scope of the issue.
* 10:08 — L2 SRE: Hiro took over the investigation, focusing on the metrics pipeline and potential issues with data ingestion.

## Diagnosis steps and hypotheses
Initial hypotheses centered around a potential misconfiguration in the metrics pipeline or an issue with the data storage. To investigate this, the `tsdb-cli check --config /etc/tsdb.conf` command was run to verify the configuration of the time-series database. This step was crucial in identifying any discrepancies in the configuration that could lead to the ingestion pipeline failure.

## Resolution steps
1. Restart metrics-agent with --debug flag
2. Set tsdb.retention.period to 30d in /etc/tsdb.conf
3. Run tsdb-cli repair on node-1 with --force option

## Post-mortem note
The metrics ingestion pipeline failed due to a tsdb configuration mismatch. This incident highlighted the importance of regularly reviewing and validating the configuration of critical components like the time-series database to prevent such mismatches. Regular checks and automated validation scripts could help mitigate similar issues in the future.
