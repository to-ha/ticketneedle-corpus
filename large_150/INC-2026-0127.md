## Symptom
The incident began with a noticeable increase in write latency on one of the storage volumes, specifically after a snapshot was taken. This increase in latency was observed through monitoring tools and reported by users experiencing slow performance. Further investigation revealed that the latency spike was not limited to a single instance but occurred consistently after each snapshot, suggesting a correlation between the snapshot process and the write latency issue.

As the team delved deeper, they found that the affected volume was part of a larger storage system managed by the backup orchestrator. The orchestrator's role in managing backups and snapshots made it a prime suspect in the investigation. Tools like `iostat` and `smartctl` were utilized to gather more detailed information about the disk performance and health, respectively. However, these initial observations primarily pointed towards an issue related to the backup and snapshot process rather than a hardware failure.

The focus then shifted towards understanding how the backup orchestrator could be causing this latency. Given that write latency jumped after a snapshot, it hinted at a potential resource bottleneck or misconfiguration within the backup orchestrator's management of concurrent backups.

## Diagnosis steps and hypotheses
Initial hypotheses included a potential misconfiguration of the backup orchestrator or insufficient resources allocated for handling concurrent backups. To investigate further, the team executed the command ```
multipath -ll -d
``` to assess the multipath configuration and identify any issues that could contribute to the latency. However, this did not reveal any configuration problems that would directly explain the observed latency.

Given the nature of the issue, the team also considered the possibility of disk queue depth being too low, leading to bottlenecks during snapshot operations. This hypothesis was explored by examining the current settings and comparing them against recommended best practices for similar storage systems.

## Resolution steps
1. Restart backup orchestrator service with --force flag
2. Set max_parallel_backups to 4 in /etc/backup/config.yaml
3. Increase disk queue depth to 32 using multipath -ll command

## Post-mortem note
The backup orchestrator failed to manage concurrent backups due to insufficient resources. This incident highlights the importance of regularly reviewing and adjusting configuration settings based on the system's workload, especially for critical components like the backup orchestrator. Future preventive measures will include periodic checks on the backup orchestrator's performance and resource utilization to avoid similar incidents.
