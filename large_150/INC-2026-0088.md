- **Ticket ID:** INC-2026-0088
- **Reported:** 2026-02-27 10:55 UTC
- **Priority:** P2

- **Status:** Resolved

## Symptom
The storage team noticed that the lifecycle policy was deleting objects from the cold-tier archive earlier than expected. This was first observed through monitoring alerts, which indicated an unusual spike in deletion activity. Upon further investigation, it became clear that this was not an isolated incident but rather a persistent issue affecting a significant portion of the archived data.

## Escalation timeline
- 11:09 — L1 Platform support: Alice
  Alice immediately began reviewing the monitoring logs to understand the scope of the issue.
- 11:41 — L2 Platform engineer: Aisha
  Aisha was tasked with diving deeper into the configuration of the lifecycle policy to identify any potential misconfigurations.

## Diagnosis steps and hypotheses
Initial hypotheses suggested a misconfiguration in the lifecycle policy or an issue with the metrics platform's reporting. To investigate, the team used `iostat` to monitor disk utilization and ran 
```
iostat -d /dev/sda 10 5
```
to assess the disk activity, which helped rule out any issues with disk performance. However, the root cause remained elusive until a detailed review of the archive policy settings was conducted.

## Resolution steps
1. Set archive policy retention days to 365
2. Run s3-cli sync on /mnt/archive with --delete flag
3. Verify iostat output for disk utilization below 80%
4. Adjust smartctl scheduled scan interval to 86400 seconds

## Post-mortem note
The archive lifecycle policy was not properly configured to manage storage capacity. This oversight led to the premature deletion of archived objects, highlighting the importance of regular audits of policy configurations to prevent such incidents in the future.
