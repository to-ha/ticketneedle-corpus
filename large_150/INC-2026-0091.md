## Symptom
The incident began with reports of dashboard staleness, where metrics were not being updated in real-time. This was first observed by the monitoring team, who noticed that the metrics ingestion pipeline was experiencing a significant lag. As the issue persisted, it became clear that the problem was not limited to a single dashboard or metric, but rather a systemic issue affecting the entire monitoring infrastructure. The metrics ingestion lag was causing delays in alerting and notification, which in turn was impacting the ability of the operations team to respond to incidents in a timely manner. Further investigation revealed that the tracing collector was not sending data to the metrics platform, which was contributing to the ingestion lag.

The symptoms of the issue were initially subtle, with only a small subset of metrics being affected. However, as the issue progressed, the number of affected metrics grew, and the lag time increased. The monitoring team attempted to troubleshoot the issue using various tools, including alert-history and promtool-equivalent, but were unable to identify the root cause. The issue was eventually escalated to the L1 SRE on-call, who began a more in-depth investigation.

As the investigation continued, it became clear that the issue was related to the tracing collector, which was not functioning correctly. The tracing collector is responsible for collecting and forwarding metrics data to the metrics platform, and its failure was causing the ingestion lag. The team attempted to restart the tracing collector, but this did not resolve the issue. The problem persisted, and the team was forced to continue troubleshooting.

The user impact of the issue was significant, as the dashboard staleness was causing delays in incident response and resolution. The operations team was unable to rely on the metrics data, which was impacting their ability to make informed decisions. The issue was also causing frustration among the development team, who were unable to monitor the performance of their applications.

The monitoring team continued to investigate the issue, using tools such as tsdb-cli to inspect the metrics data. They discovered that the tracing collector was experiencing issues with memory allocation, which was causing it to fail. However, the root cause of the issue was still unknown, and further investigation was needed.

## Diagnosis steps and hypotheses
The team considered several hypotheses during the investigation, including issues with the metrics platform, problems with the tracing collector configuration, and network connectivity issues. However, each of these hypotheses was ruled out through a combination of diagnostic tests and evidence review.

One hypothesis was that the issue was related to the metrics platform itself, rather than the tracing collector. To test this hypothesis, the team used promtool-equivalent to inspect the metrics data and verify that it was being received correctly. However, this did not reveal any issues with the metrics platform.

Another hypothesis was that the tracing collector configuration was incorrect, causing it to fail to send data to the metrics platform. To test this hypothesis, the team reviewed the tracing collector configuration files and verified that they were correct. However, this did not reveal any issues with the configuration.

The team also considered the possibility of network connectivity issues, which could be preventing the tracing collector from sending data to the metrics platform. To test this hypothesis, the team used tools such as `dig` and `tcpdump` to verify that the network connectivity was functioning correctly. However, this did not reveal any issues with the network connectivity.

As the investigation continued, the team began to suspect that the issue was related to the tracing collector itself, rather than the metrics platform or network connectivity. To test this hypothesis, the team used `tsdb-cli` to inspect the metrics data and verify that it was being collected correctly.
```
tsdb-cli inspect --path /var/lib/tsdb
```
This revealed issues with the tracing collector's memory allocation, which was causing it to fail.

The team eventually discovered that the tracing collector had run out of memory due to excessive sample collection. This was causing the tracing collector to fail, which in turn was causing the metrics ingestion lag. The root cause of the issue was finally identified, and the team was able to develop a plan to resolve it.

## Resolution steps
1. Restart tracing collector on node 12
2. Set max_samples to 1000 in /etc/tracing/config.yaml
3. Increase memory limit to 4G for tracing collector pod
4. Update tsdb retention period to 30d using tsdb-cli

## Post-mortem note
The tracing collector ran out of memory due to excessive sample collection. This issue was missed initially because the symptoms were subtle and the root cause was not immediately apparent. In hindsight, the team should have investigated the tracing collector's memory allocation earlier in the investigation. To prevent similar issues in the future, the team will implement additional monitoring and alerting around the tracing collector's memory usage. The team will also review the tracing collector's configuration to ensure that it is optimized for the current workload. Additionally, the team will develop a plan to handle excessive sample collection, including implementing measures to prevent it from occurring in the first place.
