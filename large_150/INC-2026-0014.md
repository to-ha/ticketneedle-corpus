## Symptom
The object storage bucket replication lag was observed to be growing past the SLA threshold. This was initially detected by monitoring tools, which alerted the team to a potential issue. Further investigation revealed that the replication queue was increasing in size, indicating a problem with the replication process.

As the incident progressed, users began to report delays in accessing recently uploaded objects, confirming that the replication lag was impacting service performance. The team focused on identifying the root cause of the replication lag, considering factors such as network connectivity, storage capacity, and system configuration.

The symptom was characterized by a steady increase in replication lag over a short period, suggesting a sudden change or failure in the system. The team's initial hypothesis was that the issue might be related to a recent configuration change or a hardware failure, but further diagnosis was needed to confirm this.

## Diagnosis steps and hypotheses
The team considered several hypotheses, including network connectivity issues, storage capacity problems, and system configuration errors. To investigate these hypotheses, the team used tools such as `fio` and `iostat` to analyze disk performance and `s3-cli` to inspect the replication queue.

One potential solution considered was to adjust the replication timeout using the command:
```
fio --replicate-bucket --timeout 300
```
However, this was not initially attempted, and the team focused on gathering more data before making changes to the system configuration.

## Resolution steps
1. Set bucket replication timeout to 300 seconds
2. Increase worker count to 5 for replication task
3. Restart object storage service on node 10.0.1.100
4. Verify replication queue size with s3-cli --queue-size 1000

## Post-mortem note
The object storage bucket replication timed out due to insufficient worker nodes. This incident highlights the importance of monitoring system performance and adjusting configuration parameters as needed to prevent similar issues in the future.
